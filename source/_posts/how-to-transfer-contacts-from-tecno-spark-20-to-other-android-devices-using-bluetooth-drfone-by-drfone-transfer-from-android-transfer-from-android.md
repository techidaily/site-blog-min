Step 2: Keep the Tecno Spark 20 devices close by, say, on the table. This will not work if the Tecno Spark 20 devices are in different rooms or too far away.

Step 3: Launch Smart Switch on both the Tecno Spark 20 devices

Step 4: Tap Send Data on old Android

Step 5: Tap Receive Data on the new Samsung device

Step 7: Tap Allow on your old device to start the transfer process. Don’t worry, this will not dump all your content just yet.
