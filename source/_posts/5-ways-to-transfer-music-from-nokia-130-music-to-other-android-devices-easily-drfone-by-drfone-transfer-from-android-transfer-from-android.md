**Method 1:** The first method is to swipe from top to down on your Nokia 130 Music to view the swipe menu on some Android OS. You would be able to view and immediately turn on Bluetooth with a single click.

**Method 2:** Go to “Connection” from the Settings Menu on your Nokia 130 Music, and then in the Connections options, you will see “Bluetooth”. Make sure it is switched on. ## Part 4. How to transfer music from Nokia 130 Music to Other Android Devices using NFC?

NFC or Near Field Communication is another wireless means to transfer music from Nokia 130 Music to Other Android Devices. Although, unlike Bluetooth, this method requires contact between the two devices making the transfer.

**Below are the steps on how to transfer music from Nokia 130 Music to Other Android Devices using NFC.**

**Step 1.** First, enable NFC connection on both the Nokia 130 Music devices between which you want to transfer the music files. To switch on NFC on Android, go to Phone’s “Settings” and click on “More Settings” under “Wireless and Network” options. Now click on the NFC button to ensure that it is switched On. Do the same on the other Android device as well.
