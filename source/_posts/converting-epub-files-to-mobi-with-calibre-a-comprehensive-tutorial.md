**2\.** Want to read our favorite EPUB books on Kindle device or Kindle app.

**4.** Mobi is the most common eBook format of Amazon Kindle.

**5.** Calibre is a free and powerful eBook management tool, which can help us convert multiple eBook formats, including EPUB to Mobi.
