### Solution 1. Using ShareIt App

When Infinix Smart 8 Plus Migrate is not an option, ShareIt can be a versatile alternative for **Infinix Smart 8 Plus to iPhone transfer**. ShareIt is a popular cross-platform file-sharing application known for its simplicity and efficiency. To use it, you'll need to download the ShareIt app on both your Infinix Smart 8 Plus and iPhone.

If you have downloaded and installed it, you can continue with the steps below:

- **Step 1:** Connect your Infinix Smart 8 Plus and iPhone devices

Since ShareIt operates by creating a hotspot network among devices, it's necessary to have both devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network. Make sure to keep both devices close to each other, too.

- **Step 2:** Select the files to transfer

On your Infinix Smart 8 Plus device, click the "Send" button. After a new window appears displaying all the categorized files on your device, choose the specific files you want to transfer.

- **Step 3:** Look for the Infinix Smart 8 Plus device to transfer

After selecting the files, press "Send" and provide the necessary permissions for GPS and Bluetooth. Once permissions are granted, the Infinix Smart 8 Plus device will initiate a search for nearby devices.

- **Step 4:** Transfer the files to the iPhone.
