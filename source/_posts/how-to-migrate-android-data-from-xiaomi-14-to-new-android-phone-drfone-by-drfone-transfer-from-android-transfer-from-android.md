## Part 1: How to migrate Android using Google Drive?

Since Google Drive is already available on all the Xiaomi 14 devices, it can readily be used to migrate Android to Android without much trouble. Firstly, you need to sync your data from the source device and later log-in to the same account to sync your new phone with the Drive. To perform phone migration using Google Drive, follow these steps:

1\. To start with, go to Settings > Backup & Reset on the source device and turn on the option of “Backup my Data”.
