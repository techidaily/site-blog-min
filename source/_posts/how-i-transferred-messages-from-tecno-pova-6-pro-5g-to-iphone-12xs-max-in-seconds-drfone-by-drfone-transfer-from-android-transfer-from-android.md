The first method to transfer text messages from Tecno Pova 6 Pro 5G to iPhone 12/XS (Max) is using the official Move to iOS app from Apple. **Guide for Move to iOS** **to transfer messages from Tecno Pova 6 Pro 5G to iPhone 12/XS (Max)**
