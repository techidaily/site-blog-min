Syncing contacts to a Google account serves as a convenient method. It can automatically transfer contacts from an Android device to an iPhone. Follow these instructions to sync your contacts to your Google account and subsequently access them on your iPhone:

- **Step 1.** Access the Google Contacts app on your Vivo Y78 5G and tap on the “Profile” icon at the top right corner. Following this, access the "Contacts Settings" option followed by "Google Contacts sync settings."
