### File Size

The size of files being transferred affects the process in general. This is because the Samsung Galaxy A05 device on the receiving end needs to allocate the data individually to the available space. The type of files involved in the data transfer is also a factor. Why so? The simplest explanation is that compatibility matters a lot in any form of file transfer. For instance, the procedure will likely fail if the receiver’s device does not support the data you’re trying to send. But don’t worry. There are lots of methods to transfer data from Samsung Galaxy A05 to iPhone 15 if your first try doesn’t work out.

Device storage is definitely the first thing you should check before going ahead with the data transfer. It is important that the receiver’s device has adequate memory to accommodate the incoming files. Otherwise, the transfer will likely not push through.
