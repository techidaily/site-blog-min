## Part 3: How to Transfer Photos from Poco M6 Pro 4G to iPhone via Bluetooth

- If you are thinking about **how to transfer pictures from Poco M6 Pro 4G to iPhone**, then follow the process outlined below.
- **1.** Open up Bluetooth on both Android and iOS devices. Both the smartphones should have the option to turn Bluetooth on/off via a sliding panel from the top of the screen.
- **2.** Now, tap on the checkbox to make the Poco M6 Pro 4G device visible to other mobile devices.
- **3.** On your Poco M6 Pro 4G> Go to your File Manager > DCIM Folder (for Camera content).
