**Step 1.** There are two ways you can turn on Bluetooth on your Vivo Y27 5G

**Method 1:** The first method is to swipe from top to down on your Vivo Y27 5G to view the swipe menu on some Android OS. You would be able to view and immediately turn on Bluetooth with a single click.
