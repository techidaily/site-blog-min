## Part 1: Transfer Data From Huawei P60 to iPhone 15/14/13 Using Move To iOS

The easiest way for most people is to use the Move to iOS app that Apple created specifically for switching from Android. Here's how:

**Step 1:** On your Huawei P60, download Move to iOS from the Google Play Store.

**Step 2:** When setting up your new iPhone 15/14/13, select the option to transfer data from Android.

**Step 3:** Open the Move to iOS app on your Huawei P60 and tap Continue.

**Step 4:** Wait for a 6 or 10-digit code to appear on your iPhone and enter it on your Huawei P60.

![transfer information from iphone to android](https://images.wondershare.com/drfone/article/2024/02/how-can-you-transfer-data-from-android-to-iphone-13-5.png)

**Step 6:** On Android, select the content you want to transfer like contacts, messages, photos, etc.

**Step 7:** Wait for the transfer process to finish, then tap Done on Android and Continue on iPhone.
