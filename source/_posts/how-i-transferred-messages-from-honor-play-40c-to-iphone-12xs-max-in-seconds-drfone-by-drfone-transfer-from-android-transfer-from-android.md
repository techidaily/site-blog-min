**Method 1:** The first method is to swipe from top to down on your Honor Play 40C to view the swipe menu on some Android OS. You would be able to view and immediately turn on Bluetooth with a single click.

**Method 2:** Go to “Connection” from the Settings Menu on your Honor Play 40C, and then in the Connections options, you will see “Bluetooth”. Make sure it is switched on.
