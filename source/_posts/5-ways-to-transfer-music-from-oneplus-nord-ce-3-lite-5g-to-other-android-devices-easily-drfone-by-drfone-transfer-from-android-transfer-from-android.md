**Below are the steps on how to transfer music from OnePlus Nord CE 3 Lite 5G to Other Android Devices using NFC.**

**Step 1.** First, enable NFC connection on both the OnePlus Nord CE 3 Lite 5G devices between which you want to transfer the music files. Do the same on the other Android device as well.
