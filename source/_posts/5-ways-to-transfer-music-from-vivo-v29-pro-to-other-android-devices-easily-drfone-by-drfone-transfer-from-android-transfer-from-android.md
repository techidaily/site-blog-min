**Here are the steps to follow to know how to transfer music from Vivo V29 Pro to Other Android Devices.**

**Step 1.** There are two ways you can turn on Bluetooth on your Vivo V29 Pro

**Method 1:** The first method is to swipe from top to down on your Vivo V29 Pro to view the swipe menu on some Android OS. You would be able to view and immediately turn on Bluetooth with a single click.

**Method 2:** Go to “Connection” from the Settings Menu on your Vivo V29 Pro, and then in the Connections options, you will see “Bluetooth”. Make sure it is switched on. Also, ensure that the phone’s Bluetooth visibility is enabled so that your device can be seen and easily paired with the other device.

## Part 4. How to transfer music from Vivo V29 Pro to Other Android Devices using NFC?

NFC or Near Field Communication is another wireless means to transfer music from Vivo V29 Pro to Other Android Devices. Although, unlike Bluetooth, this method requires contact between the two devices making the transfer.

**Below are the steps on how to transfer music from Vivo V29 Pro to Other Android Devices using NFC.**

**Step 1.** First, enable NFC connection on both the Vivo V29 Pro devices between which you want to transfer the music files. To switch on NFC on Android, go to Phone’s “Settings” and click on “More Settings” under “Wireless and Network” options. Now click on the NFC button to ensure that it is switched On. Do the same on the other Android device as well.
