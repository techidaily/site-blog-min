It is common for Mac users to get some movies/videos in AVI format, either from the internet, digital cameras or shared from friends who use Windows. Most of people wanna convert AVI to QuickTime MOV Mac for playback or editing with ease since their [AVI videos can't be recognized by QuickTime](https://tools.techidaily.com/macxdvd/products/). As is known to all, AVI (Audio Video Interleaved) is a multimedia container format introduced by Microsoft and can contain both audio and video data. The common video codecs of AVI include DivX, Xvid, MJPEG, MPEG-4, MPEG-3, MPEG-1, MPEG-H, etc. There are many Windows-based media players supporting replaying AVI format video, but you can't play AVI on Mac, QuickTime, iTunes or other iOS devices. Specifically, you will encounter no sound and get an error message stating "QuickTime Player can't open avi." 

QuickTime, a popular media player developed by Apple Inc, can stream video formatted with [MOV](https://tools.techidaily.com/macxdvd/products/), MP4, M4V, etc. BTW, QuickTime only can play partial AVI file with Motion JPEG video. In other words, to play all sorts of AVI videos on QuickTime, you need to convert AVI to MOV to be compatible with QuickTime.
