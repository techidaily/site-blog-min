When the "Location Not Available" message appears on Find My Friends, it means that you have disabled location sharing or have turned off your device's location services. **_Does "Location Not Available" mean that there is something wrong with Vivo Y100 5G ?_**

Not! This message does not necessarily mean that you have blocked yourself or there is something wrong with your device. [**_How to distinguish "No Location Found" and "Location Not Available"?_**](https://drfone.wondershare.com/virtual-location/no-location-found-vs-location-not-available.html)

"No Location Found" means that the Find My app is unable to locate the Vivo Y100 5G device being tracked. This can happen for a variety of reasons, such as if the Vivo Y100 5G device is turned off, not connected to the internet, or if location services are disabled.
