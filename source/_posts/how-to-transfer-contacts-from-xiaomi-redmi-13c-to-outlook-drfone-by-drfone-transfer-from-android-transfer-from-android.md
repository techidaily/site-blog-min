## How to Transfer Content from android to iPhone Directly

Since Xiaomi Redmi 13C is embracing Android as its operating system, transitioning from an iPhone to a Xiaomi Redmi 13C, or vice versa, can pose challenges due to the differing operating systems. But it doesn't mean impossible!

## Part 1. How to Transfer Content from Xiaomi Redmi 13C to iPhone Directly

You must be familiar with Xiaomi Redmi 13C Migrate if you are a Xiaomi Redmi 13C user. It is a solution for seamless data transfer used in Xiaomi Redmi 13C devices. With this app, you can transfer data from your Xiaomi Redmi 13C device to others, such as iPhones.

However, most of the latest Xiaomi Redmi 13C models are now unable to run Xiaomi Redmi 13C Migrate since it's a pretty outdated app. It has been retired by the company since 2015. Users are seeking alternative methods for data transfer as a result. In this section, we explore more effective solutions to transfer content directly from Xiaomi Redmi 13C to iPhone besides Xiaomi Redmi 13C Migrate.

### Solution 1. Using ShareIt App

When Xiaomi Redmi 13C Migrate is not an option, ShareIt can be a versatile alternative for **Xiaomi Redmi 13C to iPhone transfer**. ShareIt is a popular cross-platform file-sharing application known for its simplicity and efficiency. To use it, you'll need to download the ShareIt app on both your Xiaomi Redmi 13C and iPhone.

If you have downloaded and installed it, you can continue with the steps below:

- **Step 1:** Connect your Xiaomi Redmi 13C and iPhone devices

Since ShareIt operates by creating a hotspot network among devices, it's necessary to have both devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network. Make sure to keep both devices close to each other, too.

- **Step 2:** Select the files to transfer

On your Xiaomi Redmi 13C device, click the "Send" button. After a new window appears displaying all the categorized files on your device, choose the specific files you want to transfer.

- **Step 3:** Look for the Xiaomi Redmi 13C device to transfer

After selecting the files, press "Send" and provide the necessary permissions for GPS and Bluetooth. Once permissions are granted, the Xiaomi Redmi 13C device will initiate a search for nearby devices.

- **Step 4:** Transfer the files to the iPhone.

On your iPhone, open the ShareIt app and click "Receive." This action opens all the necessary ports to receive shared files. When your Xiaomi Redmi 13C phone detects your iPhone, tap on the iPhone’s avatar to proceed with the transfer.

## Part 3. How to Do Xiaomi Redmi 13C to iPhone Data Transfer Using Dropbox/Google Drive

Another method for **Xiaomi Redmi 13C to iPhone transfer** is using Cloud services like Dropbox or Google Drive. Both Dropbox and Google Drive serve as virtual storage spaces accessible from any device with an internet connection. Users can upload and store their files to the cloud, making them available for download on other devices.

Either Google Drive or Dropbox follows similar steps for data transfer. The difference between them is in the amount of free storage each service provides. Notably, Google Drive provides 15GB of free storage, while Dropbox offers 2GB.

Follow these steps on how to do a Xiaomi Redmi 13C to iPhone transfer via cloud storage services.

- **Step 1:** Install and set up Dropbox/Google Drive:

Begin by installing the Dropbox or Google Drive app on both your Xiaomi Redmi 13C and iPhone. Create accounts if you don't have them already.

- **Step 2:** Upload the files from Xiaomi Redmi 13C

Open the cloud storage app on your Xiaomi Redmi 13C device and upload the data you want to transfer. This can include photos, videos, documents, and more. Then, wait for the upload to complete. Make sure that all files are successfully uploaded before proceeding.

- **Step 3:** Sign in to the cloud storage on your iPhone

On your iPhone, open Dropbox or Google Drive and sign in with the same account. Find the transferred files in the cloud storage.

- **Step 4:** Download to iPhone:

Select the files you want to transfer and download them to your iPhone. Once the download is complete, organize the transferred files on your iPhone as needed.
