This app is designed to streamline the transfer process, allowing you to move not only your text messages but also other essential data to your iPhone.

To use this Move to iOS app, you need to download it from the Google Play Store first on your OnePlus Open. Then, follow the steps to **transfer SMS from OnePlus Open to iPhone** here:

- **Step 1:** Setup your iPhone. Power up your new iPhone 14 or 15 and progress through the initial setup until you reach the "Apps & Data" screen. At this point, select the option that reads "Move Data from Android."

- **Step 3:** Enter the Code. On your OnePlus Open, a screen will appear, requesting a 6–10-digit code. This code will be generated on your iPhone. Enter the displayed code on your OnePlus Open to make a secure connection between the two devices.
