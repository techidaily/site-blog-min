  MP4 is among Xiaomi Redmi A2 supported video formats, however, some users still encounter incompatibility issues when playing MP4 files on Xiaomi Redmi A2. Why does this happen? The reason is that MP4 is a container format which may contain multiple video and audio streams encoded with various codecs. Although Xiaomi Redmi A2 is compatible with MP4 video files, it also requires the MP4 file to be encoded in the way that is recognized by your particular device model. Some MP4 videos will not play on Xiaomi Redmi A2 due to the failure of codec recognition. In that case, you will need to convert the files to Xiaomi Redmi A2 supported H.264 MP4 format.
