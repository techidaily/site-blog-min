Many audiobook lovers choose to purchase audiobooks from Audible.com. However, audiobooks purchased from Audible are protected by Audible DRM and can be listened to on limited devices and players. Wondering how to break the Audible DRM restriction? Check out [Converting Audible to MP3](https://tools.techidaily.com/epubor/products/) or M4B, which is the best option available. There are different methods to convert Audible books to MP3, but not all tools can preserve your Audible chapter information. In this guide, I will show you the easiest way to maintain chapter information while converting an Audible book.

#### Preparation before converting Audible books: Download Audible files in AAX format.

![Download Audible Books](http://www.epubor.com/images/uppic/download-audible-books.png)

Log in to your Audible account and click on "Library", you will see all your purchased Audible books. Usually, there are two Audio quality options-**Enhanced** and **Format 4**. Please select "Enhanced" quality to enable all your downloaded books in .aax format.

**Updated 08/31/2020: Amazon has removed audible format 4 option and moves to [Audible enhanced format](https://tools.techidaily.com/epubor/products/) only at the end of July in 2020.**

Note: Only when your downloaded Audible books are in AAX format will the chapter information be preserved when converting to M4B. If your downloaded Audible books are in AA format, the converted M4B file will not retain the chapter information. However, there is no need to worry about this, as all Audible books downloaded to your computer are now in AAX format.
