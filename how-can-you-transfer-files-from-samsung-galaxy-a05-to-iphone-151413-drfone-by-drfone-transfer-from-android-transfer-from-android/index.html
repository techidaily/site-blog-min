You must be familiar with Samsung Galaxy A05 Migrate if you are a Samsung Galaxy A05 user. It is a solution for seamless data transfer used in Samsung Galaxy A05 devices. With this app, you can transfer data from your Samsung Galaxy A05 device to others, such as iPhones.

However, most of the latest Samsung Galaxy A05 models are now unable to run Samsung Galaxy A05 Migrate since it’s a pretty outdated app. It has been retired by the company since 2015. In this section, we explore more effective solutions to transfer content directly from Samsung Galaxy A05 to iPhone besides Samsung Galaxy A05 Migrate.

Solution 1. Using ShareIt App

When Samsung Galaxy A05 Migrate is not an option, ShareIt can be a versatile alternative for Samsung Galaxy A05 to iPhone transfer. ShareIt is a popular cross-platform file-sharing application known for its simplicity and efficiency. To use it, you’ll need to download the ShareIt app on both your Samsung Galaxy A05 and iPhone.

If you have downloaded and installed it, you can continue with the steps below:

  • Step 1: Connect your Samsung Galaxy A05 and iPhone devices

Since ShareIt operates by creating a hotspot network among devices, it’s necessary to have both devices connected to the same Wi-Fi network. Make sure to keep both devices close to each other, too.

  • Step 2: Select the files to transfer

On your Samsung Galaxy A05 device, click the “Send” button. After a new window appears displaying all the categorized files on your device, choose the specific files you want to transfer.

  • Step 3: Look for the Samsung Galaxy A05 device to transfer

After selecting the files, press “Send” and provide the necessary permissions for GPS and Bluetooth. Once permissions are granted, the Samsung Galaxy A05 device will initiate a search for nearby devices.

  • Step 4: Transfer the files to the iPhone.

On your iPhone, open the ShareIt app and click “Receive.” This action opens all the necessary ports to receive shared files. When your Samsung Galaxy A05 phone detects your iPhone, tap on the iPhone’s avatar to proceed with the transfer.
