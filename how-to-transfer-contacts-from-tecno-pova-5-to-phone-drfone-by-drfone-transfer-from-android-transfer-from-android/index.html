On your iPod, navigate to the Settings menu and select Bluetooth. Enable Bluetooth on the Android device by accessing its settings as well. Make sure that you have set both devices to discoverable mode.

Step 2: Pair the Tecno Pova 5 devices.

Search for available Bluetooth devices on your Tecno Pova 5, and select your iPod from the list. Likewise, confirm the pairing request on your iPod.

Step 3: Initiate file transfer.

Once paired, locate the music files on your iPod that you wish to transfer. Use the sharing or send via Bluetooth option (this may vary depending on your iPod model) to send the selected music files to your Tecno Pova 5.

Step 4: Accept the files on Android.

Accept the incoming Bluetooth transfer request from the iPod. The music files will then be transferred wirelessly from your iPod to the Android device.

Step 5: Verify transfer success.

Confirm the successful transfer by checking the music library on your Tecno Pova 5. The transferred songs should now be available and ready to play.
